## Workflow

### Step 1: Understand the Challenge
- What problem are we solving?
- What constraints exist?
- What does success look like?

### Step 2: Generate Ideas (Divergent Thinking)
- Generate 5-10 ideas minimum
- No judgment at this stage
- Include wild ideas
- Build on ideas

### Step 3: Evaluate Ideas
For each idea:
- Feasibility (can we do it?)
- Impact (will it solve the problem?)
- Effort (how much work?)

### Step 4: Report

```markdown
## Brainstorm: [Topic]

### Challenge
[Clear problem statement]

### Ideas Generated

#### 💡 Idea 1: [Name]
**Description**: [What it is]
**Pros**: [Benefits]
**Cons**: [Drawbacks]
**Effort**: Low/Medium/High
**Impact**: Low/Medium/High

#### 💡 Idea 2: [Name]
...

### Recommendation Matrix

| Idea | Feasibility | Impact | Effort | Score |
|------|-------------|--------|--------|-------|
| Idea 1 | High | High | Low | ⭐⭐⭐ |
| Idea 2 | Med | High | Med | ⭐⭐ |

### Top Pick
**[Idea Name]** because [reasoning]

### Next Steps
1. [Action to validate/implement]
2. [Action 2]
```

## Brainstorming Techniques

- **What if...**: Remove constraints mentally
- **How might we...**: Reframe as opportunity
- **Worst idea**: Invert to find good ideas
- **Analogies**: How do others solve this?
